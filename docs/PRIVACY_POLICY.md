# Privacy Policy — Safe Numbers

_Last updated: [DATE]_

Safe Numbers ("we") helps families protect seniors from phone-support impersonation
scams. We collect the minimum needed to run the service, and we designed the mobile app
to work offline precisely so it doesn't have to send data anywhere.

## What we collect

**From the caregiver (web sign-up):**
- Email address and payment details — processed by **Stripe**; we never see or store
  card numbers.
- The contacts you choose to add for your loved one (name, phone number, a label like
  "Doctor," optional note), and an optional emergency contact ("Call My Person").
- If you arrived via a partner link, the referral tag on that link.

**From the senior's app:** essentially nothing.
- The app activates once with a 6-digit code, downloads its configuration, and then runs
  entirely on the device. It contains **no ads, no analytics, no trackers, and no
  location access**, and it never records who was called or when.
- Standard web-server logs (IP address, timestamp) are kept briefly when the app
  activates or refreshes, for security and abuse prevention (rate limiting).

## What we do NOT do
- We do not sell or share personal information (as defined by the CCPA/CPRA).
- We do not use contact data for advertising or profiling.
- We do not collect call history, contacts from the phone's address book, or location.

## How data is used
Only to operate the service: showing the directory and the caregiver-added contacts in
the app, managing your subscription (Stripe), and preventing abuse.

## Storage & security
Data is stored on our servers in the United States. Activation data delivered to the
device is cryptographically signed; caregiver credentials are stored hashed; access is
rate-limited and encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

## Retention & deletion
We keep account data while the account is active. Email us to delete an account and all
its contacts; deletion completes within 30 days (billing records are retained as Stripe
and tax law require).

## Children
The service is for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
